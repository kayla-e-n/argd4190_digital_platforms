TV Extras

Psych

Psych TV Show is a detective show where the main character, Shawn, pretends to be psychic to solve crimes for the Santa Barbara Police Department with his childhood best friend Gus. Shawn and Gus get into many funny situations trying to keep the police believing that Shawn in psychic. They sneak into many different crime scenes as Shawn investigates to use the clues he finds to show-off to the police. Psych is a comedy interpretation of Sherlock Holmes being a detective duo with one being a highly observant person and their partner being in the medical field. The entire cast, including all of the police officers and Shawn's dad are very loveable and deep characters. At surface level this show seems like a comedy detective show, but it has become much more the more you watch it.




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *

Steins;Gate

Steins Gate is an anime about time travel and alternate timelines that messes with your head on what is actually happening. We follow a goofy scientist who says random words and is definitely going a little crazy as a scientist trying to create a time machine. He has a small team of a childhood friend and a random guy for computer work. One day our crazy scientist goes to see another scientist talk about time travel when timelines start colliding and us as the viewers get more and more confused on what's going on. However the show keeps you on your toes every episode and has many moments at the end




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *

Alice in Borderland

Alice in Borderland is an adaptation of Alice in Wonderland but it has taken a deadly turn in an apocolypic "Wonderland". Arisu and his friends run around Shibuya after skipping work and hear fireworks, so they run into the station bathroom and when they come out all the people have disappeared. The city looks abandoned now and there are flying playing cards in the sky. As they get closer to these cards they have to play a game for life or death. This is a show that has really loveable characters and really fun death games they have to play. Every season that is announced I get more and more excited because I am at the edge of my seat.




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *

Avater: The Last Airbender

Avatar the Last Airbender is a cartoon following Aang, the Avatar who is only 12 years old after he was frozen for 100 years as he and his friends try to stop the ongoing war against the fire nation. Once Aang is awoken from the ice he soon realizes how bad the war has gotten in the 100 years that have passed. He is then on a task with his new friends to master all four elements as an avatar should to defeat the fire lord.




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *
