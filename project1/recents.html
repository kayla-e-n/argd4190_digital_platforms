Most Recent films

Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender

Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender follows the Gaang in a pursuit of a possible lead to bringing the airbenders back into the world after a Republic City as been created. The movie was very nostalgic for me, giving plenty of fan-service like the cabbage man and more. The plot was pretty interesting and did make sense canonically, since it takes place between ATLA and Legend of Korra. I do sometimes forget that this is from a kids show, so the message and plot were pretty predictable.




Rating: * * * * .5 / * * * * *

Zoolander

Zoolander is a comedy parody movie about male models and their stereotype of being dumb and their ease of being manipulated. We follow Derek Zoolander as he loses his spot as the best male model to a newer model named Hansel. Meanwhile in the background of this story, the evil villain is trying to hypnotise Zoolander to kill the prime minister of Taiwan. It is a very silly movie with a lot of funny model jokes and some inappropriate humor that is for the time of the movie being made in the early 2000s. To me it was a silly movie but very stupid and the humor didn't hit as hard for me, but it was a fun watch with friends.




Rating: * * .5 / * * * * *

Everything, Everywhere, All at Once

Everything, Everywhere, All at Once is an amazing film about succumming to the feeling of nothingness in a world of infinite possibilities. We follow a Chinese family as their reality begins to merge with each other as our main character Evelyn figures out how to channel other versions of herself. A version of her daughter is causing chaos in the cosmos and she also meets alternatives of her husband, father, and tax lady. I found this movie really moving. Not only is is beautifully shot and acted, but the premise seems confusing at first but is very relatable. There are a lot of notions to hong Kong cinema which I really enjoy because I have somewhat recently been introduced to that genre of film. I thought it had really funny moments as well as moments to make you tear up.




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *

But I'm a Cheerleader

But I'm a Cheerleader is a gay romance movie about a lesbian being sent to a conversion therapy camp to become straight again. We follow a young high school cheerleader who's family, friends, and boyfriend give an intervention to about her being a lesbian. She is then sent to a conversion camp for gay people. There she meets a steryotypical group of different LGBTQ+ people and counselors alike. She goes through trials to accept her sexuality and eventually falls in love as well. This was a really funny movie that was really fun and campy. I thought it was hilarious to see RuPaul be a "straight" man and I thought the main couple was really cute.




Rating: * * * * .5 / * * * * *
