Top 4 Favorite Films

The Princess Bride

The Princess Bride by Rob Reiner is my favorite film because it has a little bit of every genre and it is the perfect type of humor that I absolutely love! This is my childhood favorite that still persists to this day!

Grand Budapest Hotel

The Grand Budapest Hotel by Wes Anderson is a comedy/adventure film about a hotel manager and his lobby boy as they deal with some particularly unforgiving patrons.

Rear Window

Rear Window by Alfred Hitchcock is a beautiful suspense film about a photo-journalist who is stuck at home with a broken leg when he thinks he sees one of his neighbors commit a crime.

The Thing

The Thing by John Carpenter is a horror movie about an alien creature infesting a research crew as they are stuck in the Arctic.
