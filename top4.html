Top 4 Favorite Films

The Princess Bride

The Princess Bride by Rob Reiner is my favorite film because it has a little bit of every genre and it is the perfect type of humor that I absolutely love! This is my childhood favorite that still persists to this day! We follow Princess Buttercup as she is seperated from her true love Wesley. Eventually she gets engaged the the prince (unhappily) and she gets kidnapped by a group of paid mercenaries. After she is kidnapped she is being followed by the Prince as well as a mysterious man in black. This movie has some really funny moments, romance, tears, action, and more. Basically everything you could want in a movie is in this movie, which is why it's still my favorite. It is similar in a lot of ways to the book, but its also different in ways too.




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *

Grand Budapest Hotel

The Grand Budapest Hotel by Wes Anderson is a comedy/adventure film about a hotel manager and his lobby boy as they deal with some particularly unforgiving patrons. The manager of the Grand Budapest Hotel has a scandelous relationship with one of his older patrons, and one evening she passes away. He is invited begrudgingly to her wake and will reading where afterwards her family is after him. As he is on the run, his new Lobby Boy is tagging along and helping his boss. This film has a very dry sense of humor that I like, and it is absolutely beautiful to look at.




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *

Rear Window

Rear Window by Alfred Hitchcock is a beautiful suspense film about a photo-journalist who is stuck at home with a broken leg when he thinks he sees one of his neighbors commit a crime. After getting in an accient and breaking his entire leg, Jeff is stuck looking out his window in a poorer part of the city. He spends his days looking at all of his neighbors while his girlfriend and nurse visit periodically. Until one evening Jeff realizes that one of his neighbors get in a fight with her husband and then, she disappears... This movie is one of the most suspenseful movies I have seen in a long time. I was genuinely tensing my body because I felt so hopeless. It wasn't a scary film, but a great way of tension and suspense in a film.




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *

The Thing

The Thing by John Carpenter is a horror movie about an alien creature infesting a research crew as they are stuck in the Arctic. One day a dog is being chased by a chopper that is shooting at the dog. Our main crew saves the dog, not understanding what the foreigners were saying about the dog. We soon find out that the dog has been taken over by a mysterious alien monster. We then lose our trust in all of the crew as we have no idea who is infected and who is still human. I love a horror movie that makes me question every single character and who is who. The visual effects and make-up/puppets are pretty gorey and scary. This is my favorite horror movie I have seen yet as it doesn't rely on cheap jumpscares.




Rating: * * * * * / * * * * *
